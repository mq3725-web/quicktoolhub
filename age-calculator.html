What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is a simple online tool that calculates your age based on your date of birth. It can show your age in years, months and days.

How to Use the Age Calculator?

Enter your date of birth, select the date on which you want to calculate your age, and click the Calculate Age button.
