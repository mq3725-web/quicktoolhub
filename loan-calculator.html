About Loan Calculator

QuickToolHub's free Loan Calculator helps you estimate monthly loan payments, total repayment and total interest based on the loan amount, interest rate and loan term.

How to Use

1. Enter the loan amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the loan term in years.
4. Click "Calculate Loan".
5. Your estimated monthly payment will appear instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Loan Calculator free?

Yes. QuickToolHub's Loan Calculator is completely free to use.

What does the calculator show?

It shows the estimated monthly payment, total payment and total interest.

Are the results exact?

The results are estimates. Actual loan payments may vary depending on the lender, fees, taxes and other terms.
