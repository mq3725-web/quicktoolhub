About GPA Calculator

Our free GPA Calculator helps students calculate their Grade Point Average using course grade points and credit hours.

How to Use

1. Enter the grade point for each course.
2. Enter the credit hours for each course.
3. Add more courses if needed.
4. Click Calculate GPA.

GPA Formula

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

Quality Points = Grade Point × Credit Hours
