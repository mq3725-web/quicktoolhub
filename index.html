About BMI Calculator

Our BMI Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate your Body Mass Index using your weight and height. BMI can provide a simple indication of whether your weight is within a general healthy range.

How to Use the BMI Calculator

1. Enter your weight in kilograms (KG).
2. Enter your height in centimeters (CM).
3. Click the "Calculate BMI" button.
4. Your BMI result will be displayed instantly.

How BMI is Calculated

BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters.

Formula: BMI = Weight (KG) ÷ Height² (meters)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this BMI Calculator free?

Yes. QuickToolHub's BMI Calculator is completely free to use.

Is BMI suitable for everyone?

BMI is a general screening measure and may not accurately represent body composition for every person, such as athletes with high muscle mass.
