About Tip Calculator

Our free Tip Calculator helps you quickly calculate the tip amount and total bill. It is useful for restaurants, cafes, hotels, and everyday expenses.

How to Use

1. Enter your total bill amount.
2. Enter the tip percentage.
3. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
4. Click Calculate Tip.

Tip Calculator Formula

Tip Amount = Bill × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

Total Bill = Bill + Tip Amount

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Tip Calculator free?

Yes, it is completely free to use.

Can I split the bill between people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator will show the amount per person.
