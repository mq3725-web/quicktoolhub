About Character Counter

Our free Character Counter counts characters, characters without spaces, and words instantly. It is useful for students, writers, social media posts, and content creators.

How to Use

1. Type or paste your text into the box.
2. The character and word counts update automatically.
3. Use the Clear Text button to start again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Character Counter free?

Yes, it is completely free to use.

Does it count spaces?

Yes. The Characters count includes spaces, while Without Spaces excludes them.
