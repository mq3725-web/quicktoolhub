About BMI Calculator

Our free BMI Calculator helps you calculate your Body Mass Index using your weight and height.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (meters)

BMI Categories

Below 18.5: Underweight
18.5 – 24.9: Normal weight
25 – 29.9: Overweight
30 or above: Obesity

How to Use

1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
2. Enter your height in centimeters.
3. Click "Calculate BMI".
4. Your BMI will appear instantly.
