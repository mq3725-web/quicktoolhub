About Profit Calculator

Our free Profit Calculator helps you calculate profit amount and profit percentage quickly using the cost price and selling price.

How to Use

1. Enter the cost price of the product.
2. Enter the selling price.
3. Click Calculate Profit.
4. Your profit and profit percentage will appear instantly.

Profit Formula

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Profit Percentage = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Profit Calculator free?

Yes, it is completely free to use.

Can it calculate a loss?

Yes. If the selling price is lower than the cost price, the calculator will show the loss amount and loss percentage.
