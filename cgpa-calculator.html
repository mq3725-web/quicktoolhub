About CGPA Calculator

Our free CGPA Calculator helps students calculate their cumulative grade point average using grade points and credit hours.

How to Calculate CGPA

CGPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade point by its credit hours, adding all the results, and dividing the total by the total credit hours.

CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the grade point for each course.
2. Enter the credit hours for each course.
3. Add more courses if needed.
4. Click "Calculate CGPA".
5. Your CGPA will appear instantly.
