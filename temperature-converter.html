About Temperature Converter

Our free Temperature Converter lets you quickly convert temperatures between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

How to Use

1. Enter a temperature.
2. Select Celsius to Fahrenheit or Fahrenheit to Celsius.
3. Click Convert.
4. Your converted temperature will appear instantly.

Temperature Formulas

Celsius to Fahrenheit:
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius:
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
