About Discount Calculator

QuickToolHub's free Discount Calculator helps you calculate how much money you save and what the final price will be after applying a discount.

How to Use

1. Enter the original price.
2. Enter the discount percentage.
3. Click "Calculate Discount".
4. The discount amount and final price will appear instantly.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount
